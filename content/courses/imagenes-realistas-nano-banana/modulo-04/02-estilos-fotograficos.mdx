## Por qué nombrar el estilo

Cuando nombras un estilo fotográfico reconocible, activas un conjunto predefinido de características en el modelo:
- Tipo de iluminación típica de ese estilo
- Composición habitual
- Color grading asociado
- Contexto en que se usa

"Editorial photography" activa cosas diferentes a "documentary photography" aunque ambas sean "fotografía de personas".

---

## Estilo 1: Fashion / Editorial

**Qué es:** fotografía de moda y editorial de revista. Composición muy cuidada, iluminación perfecta, sujeto como objeto de deseo visual.

**Palabras clave:**
```
fashion editorial photography, Vogue-style, high-fashion,
model pose, fashion magazine, campaign photography,
Alexander McQueen aesthetics, fashion week backstage
```

**Prompt completo:**
```
[L1] androgynous model, sharp cheekbones, avant-garde makeup,
oversized dark structured jacket

[L2] minimalist white studio, single dramatic shadow,
stark white seamless background

[L3] shot on Canon EOS R5, 50mm f/2.8, hard directional studio light
from 45 degrees, strong shadows, high contrast

[L4] high contrast black and white, deep blacks, bright whites,
fashion editorial look, no grain
```

---

## Estilo 2: Documentary / Street

**Qué es:** fotografía documental y callejera. Autenticidad máxima, momentos capturados, sin poses.

**Palabras clave:**
```
documentary photography, street photography, photojournalism,
candid moment, available light, handheld camera, real life moment,
Magnum Photos style, Cartier-Bresson inspired
```

**Prompt completo:**
```
[L1] elderly man, deeply lined face, weathered hands,
authentic smile, worn coat

[L2] busy outdoor market in Morocco, colorful spice stalls behind,
morning chaos, people moving, golden morning light

[L3] shot on Leica M11, 35mm f/2, available morning light,
handheld, slight motion blur in background, photojournalism

[L4] warm documentary color grading, visible film grain,
Kodak Tri-X inspired, high contrast, authentic and raw
```

---

## Estilo 3: Lifestyle / Brand

**Qué es:** fotografía de marca y lifestyle. Aspira a la "vida que quieres vivir". Común en e-commerce, redes sociales de marca.

**Palabras clave:**
```
lifestyle photography, brand photography, aspirational,
authentic lifestyle, brand content, social media photography,
editorial lifestyle, day in the life
```

**Prompt completo:**
```
[L1] young woman, mid-20s, wearing linen casual clothes,
relaxed natural smile, holding coffee mug

[L2] modern apartment kitchen, large windows, morning light,
plants on the counter, clean minimalist interior

[L3] shot on Sony A7R V, 50mm f/1.8, soft morning window light,
slight fill from white walls, natural authentic atmosphere

[L4] bright airy color grading, warm neutral tones,
slight warmth, clean edit, brand photography look
```

---

## Estilo 4: Fine Art Portrait

**Qué es:** retrato de arte. Busca la imagen como obra artística, no solo documental o comercial.

**Palabras clave:**
```
fine art portrait photography, Rembrandt lighting,
chiaroscuro, classical portrait, painterly photography,
studio fine art, museum-quality portrait
```

**Prompt completo:**
```
[L1] middle-aged man, strong features, weathered but dignified,
white linen shirt, thoughtful expression

[L2] dark neutral studio background, painted canvas texture,
minimal props, timeless environment

[L3] shot on Hasselblad X2D, 100mm f/2.8, Rembrandt triangle lighting,
single key light from 45 degrees, deep shadow on one side

[L4] classical painterly color grading, warm amber tones,
deep rich shadows, subtle texture in midtones, timeless quality
```

## Estilo 5: Product / Commercial

**Qué es:** fotografía de producto para e-commerce y publicidad. Claridad, precisión, el producto como protagonista absoluto.

**Palabras clave:**
```
product photography, commercial photography, still life,
product on white, studio product shot, luxury product photography,
e-commerce photography, macro product
```

**Prompt completo (producto luxury):**
```
[L1] luxury perfume bottle, dark amber glass, gold metallic accents,
slight condensation, elegant design

[L2] dark marble surface, black matte background,
single dried flower placed beside, minimal prop styling

[L3] Phase One XF, 120mm macro, single softbox from side,
backlight creating glow through amber glass,
professional product photography

[L4] dark moody commercial grading, deep shadows,
amber and gold tones, high end luxury feel
```

## Estilo 6: Analog / Film

**Qué es:** estética de película analógica. Nostalgia orgánica, imperfecciones como virtud.

**Palabras clave:**
```
analog photography, 35mm film, film photography,
grain and light leaks, Kodak Portra 400, Fujifilm 400H,
vintage film photography, expired film, lo-fi analog
```

**Prompt completo:**
```
[L1] teenager, summer clothes, candid joyful expression,
natural hair, no makeup

[L2] suburban neighborhood, summer afternoon, residential street,
golden afternoon light, suburban nostalgia

[L3] shot on Nikon FM2 film camera, 35mm f/2, afternoon sun,
handheld, natural light only, film camera feel

[L4] Kodak Portra 800 emulation, heavy film grain, light leak on edge,
warm golden tones, slight overexposure, analog feel
```

---

## Estilo 7: Dark and Moody

**Qué es:** fotografía oscura y dramática. Alto contraste, sombras profundas, atmósfera intensa.

**Palabras clave:**
```
dark moody photography, dramatic shadows, high contrast,
chiaroscuro, low-key lighting, noir photography,
dark atmospheric, cinematic dark
```

**Prompt completo:**
```
[L1] woman, 30s, intense gaze, subtle dramatic makeup,
dark clothing, mysterious expression

[L2] dimly lit bar or lounge, single lamp creating a pool of light,
dark wood and velvet, shadows dominating the frame

[L3] shot on Leica M11, 50mm f/1.4, single practical light source,
extreme shadows, low-key lighting, available dark atmosphere

[L4] dark moody color grading, deep blacks, desaturated tones,
slight cold blue in shadows, high drama, noir feel
```

---

## Estilo 8: Cinematic

**Qué es:** fotografía que parece un fotograma de película. Wide aspect ratio, color grading de cine, composición narrativa.

**Palabras clave:**
```
cinematic photography, movie still, film frame, anamorphic lens,
teal and orange color grading, cinematic composition,
movie scene, cinematic mood
```

**Prompt completo:**
```
[L1] woman walking, back to camera, long coat,
mysterious, figure in environment

[L2] wide empty boulevard in Paris at night, streetlights,
light rain, few cars, cinematic establishing shot

[L3] shot on anamorphic lens, 40mm equivalent, night available light,
practical streetlight sources, slight lens flare, cinematic 2.39:1

[L4] teal and orange color grading, deep rich blacks,
orange skin tones, teal shadows, film motion picture look --ar 21:9
```

## Combinar estilos

Los mejores resultados a veces vienen de combinar elementos de diferentes estilos:

```
# Editorial + Analog:
fashion editorial composition + Kodak Portra film grain + analog color
→ Moda con alma vintage

# Documentary + Fine Art:
candid moment + painterly lighting + classical portrait framing
→ Documental con dignidad artística

# Lifestyle + Cinematic:
authentic lifestyle moment + cinematic color grading + wide framing
→ Brand photography con profundidad narrativa
```

La clave: combina L1-L2 de un estilo con L3-L4 de otro. Mezclar todos los elementos al mismo tiempo suele crear confusión visual.
